About

Pasalubong from Japan, without the plane ticket.

Omiyage PH is a Philippine-registered importer of Japanese snacks, candy, noodles and drinks, run from a small warehouse in Pasig.

How it started

Every balikbayan box from a relative in Japan has the same layer at the top: Pocky, Kinoko no Yama, a bag of matcha KitKat minis, a few Umaibo thrown in because they are 12 yen. For most of us that layer was the only way to get the real thing. Supermarket “Japanese sections” carry export versions made elsewhere, and overseas web shops quote in dollars, take three weeks, and then the courier wants ₱600 in “customs fees” at the door.

We started Omiyage PH in 2026 to fix exactly those three things. We buy in Japan, we clear customs ourselves as a registered importer, and we sell in pesos with VAT already in the price. What you pay at checkout is what you pay.

What we promise

  • Made in Japan, bought in Japan. Our buyer in Saitama orders from the same wholesalers that supply konbini chains. We do not sell export-market versions.
  • Fresh stock. Nothing ships with less than 60 days before its best-before date. Short-dated stock goes to the Specials aisle, clearly marked.
  • Honest labels. We translate every ingredient list and allergen line. Where a product contains gelatin or alcohol, the product page says so in plain words.
  • Peso prices, VAT in, no surprises. Shipping is a flat regional rate and free over ₱2,500.
  • 7-day replacement. Crushed, melted or wrong item, and we replace or refund within 7 days of delivery.

The name

Omiyage (お土産) is the Japanese custom of bringing a boxed treat home for family and co-workers after a trip. It is the exact same instinct as pasalubong. We thought that was the right name for a shop built around it.

Registration

Omiyage PH is the trading name of Omiyage PH Trading, a sole proprietorship registered with the Department of Trade and Industry and the Bureau of Internal Revenue, with a Food and Drug Administration License to Operate as a food importer. Registration numbers appear in the footer once issued and on every official receipt.
