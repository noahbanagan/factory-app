Frequently asked questions

If it is not here, ask us.

Where do the products come from?

Japan. Our buyer orders from wholesalers in Saitama and ships by air to Manila once a month. Everything is the Japanese domestic version, not the export version sold in other Asian markets.

Are the prices really final?

Yes. Prices are in Philippine pesos and include 12% VAT. We are the importer of record and pay duties before the goods reach our warehouse, so there is no customs charge on delivery. Shipping is the only addition and it is shown in the cart before you pay.

How long does delivery take?

Orders placed before 2 PM on a business day ship the same day. Metro Manila 1–2 business days, the rest of Luzon 2–4, Visayas 3–5, Mindanao 4–7. See the shipping page for the full table.

Do you accept cash on delivery?

Yes, across Metro Manila and Luzon for orders up to ₱5,000. Visayas and Mindanao orders are prepaid by GCash, Maya, card or bank transfer.

How fresh is the stock?

We only ship items with at least 60 days left before the best-before date printed on the pack. Anything shorter goes into the Specials aisle at a discount with the date shown.

Will my chocolate melt?

From March to June we ship chocolate with an ice pack and insulated wrap, and we hold chocolate orders during a typhoon signal 3 or higher. If chocolate still arrives melted, we replace it.

Are the products halal, vegetarian or allergen-free?

We translate every manufacturer ingredient list and allergen line, and the shop filters mark items as vegetarian or no-alcohol based on those lists. None of this is a certification. Products with pork-derived gelatin, alcohol or the eight Japanese mandatory allergens are called out on each product page. For a severe allergy, email us the product name and we will photograph the actual pack.

Can I buy by the case?

Yes. Most items have a “full case” option on the product page at about 15% less per unit. For 50 units or more of anything, use the bulk order form on the contact page.

What if something arrives damaged or wrong?

Send a photo within 7 days of delivery and we replace it on the next dispatch or refund to your original payment method. Opened food cannot be returned for change of mind, in line with the Consumer Act.

Do you have a physical store?

No. We run from a warehouse in Pasig. Pickup is by appointment for bulk orders only.
