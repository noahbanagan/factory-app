Privacy policy

Effective 20 October 2026. Written to comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its Implementing Rules.

Who is responsible

Omiyage PH Trading, trading as Omiyage PH, is the personal information controller. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached through the contact page, subject line “Privacy”.

What we collect

  • To fulfil an order: name, delivery address, mobile number, email, and the items you bought.
  • To take payment: handled by our payment gateway. We never see or store full card numbers or e-wallet credentials.
  • To run the Site: browser type, pages visited, referring page and an anonymised IP, through privacy-respecting analytics. Your cart is stored in your own browser, not on our servers, until you check out.
  • If you opt in: your email for the monthly arrivals list.

Why we use it

To deliver and support your order (contract), to meet tax and customs record-keeping duties (legal obligation), to prevent fraud (legitimate interest) and to send the monthly list if you asked for it (consent, withdrawable any time by the link in each email).

Who we share it with

Only the processors needed to serve you: our couriers (name, address, mobile number), our payment gateway (amount and order reference), and our email service for the monthly list. Each is bound by a data-sharing or outsourcing agreement as required by the National Privacy Commission. We do not sell personal data.

Where it is stored and for how long

Order records are kept in the Philippines for 10 years to meet Bureau of Internal Revenue requirements. Marketing emails are kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is aggregated after 14 months.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you may ask to access, correct, or erase your data, object to processing, request a copy in a portable format, and complain to the National Privacy Commission. Write to our Data Protection Officer and we respond within 15 working days.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary storage item for your cart and region, and an analytics cookie you can decline in the banner. No advertising cookies.

Children

The Site is for buyers 18 and over. Products containing alcohol require age confirmation at delivery.

Changes

We update this policy when our practices change and post the new effective date here.
