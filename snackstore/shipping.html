Free shipping on any order of ₱2,500 or more, to every region.

Chocolate and heat

From March to June, chocolate ships with an ice pack and insulated wrap at no extra cost. During a typhoon signal 3 or higher along the route we hold chocolate orders and tell you by SMS. Everything else ships as normal unless the courier suspends service.

Damaged, wrong or missing items

Send a photo of the item and the box to our contact form within 7 days of delivery. We replace on the next dispatch or refund to your original payment method within 5 business days. Shipping on replacements is on us.

Change of mind

Unopened, non-perishable items can be returned within 7 days at your shipping cost. Opened food cannot be returned, in line with the Consumer Act of the Philippines (RA 7394).

Failed deliveries

Couriers attempt delivery twice. If the parcel comes back to us because of a wrong address or an unreachable number, we re-ship once you pay the regional rate again, including on free-shipping orders. Refused COD parcels are refunded minus the shipping fee.

Policy updated 20 October 2026.
